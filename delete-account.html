How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Stepory account at any time. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile, friend code and account settings
  • Your step history and step totals
  • Your challenges, achievements and journeys
  • Your friends list, friend requests, friend updates and cheers
  • Your group memberships and your steps in group journeys, streaks and challenges (groups you created pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the last member)

What is kept

  • Anonymous analytics and crash data held by Google Firebase, for up to 14 months. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Copies in Google's backups, until they expire in the normal backup cycle.
  • Subscription records held by Google Play, as required for billing and tax.

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open the Stepory app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the menu icon (☰) to open the navigation drawer.
  3. Tap Profile.
  4. Scroll down to the Account & Data section.
  5. Tap Delete Account (the red button with a delete icon).
  6. Read the confirmation message and what will be deleted.
  7. Confirm. If Stepory asks you to sign in again first (Google or the link sent to your email), do that and tap Delete again.

This action cannot be undone. Make sure you have exported any data you want to keep (use Export Data in Profile before deleting) and that you really want to delete your account.
