Stepory

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Stepory ("we," "our," or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android step-tracking application available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information (only if you create an account; you can also use Stepory without one)

  • Email address, when you sign in with Google or with an email link
  • Display name and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo link
  • A user ID and a short friend code (for example STEP-4K7Q9M) so friends can find you
  • A bio and other profile details you choose to add

Step and activity data

  • Step counts from your phone's step sensor and, if you turn it on, from Health Connect (read only)
  • If you are signed in, your daily step history, weekly and monthly totals, level and points are stored on our servers so they survive reinstalls and power leaderboards, leagues, groups, challenges and journeys
  • Height and weight, if you enter them, stay on your phone

Social features (only if you use them)

  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Groups you create or join, including group names, group journeys, group streaks and your step contributions to them
  • Challenge names and descriptions you write
  • Short automatic updates between friends, such as "passed you", cheers (one of four emoji) and nudges. These contain no free text
  • Which friend's invite link you joined from

Location data

  • Approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is used to show walking weather and rain reminders. It is sent to a weather service and not stored on our servers
  • If you start a journey near you, its rounded start point (about 1 km) and the place name are saved with your account
  • Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may infer an approximate location from your IP address

Usage, purchase and technical data

  • How you use the app (for example screens opened and features used) through Firebase Analytics. We do not put names, emails or messages in these events
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring
  • Subscription events (for example which plan you bought). Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card details
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID and Firebase installation ID, used for analytics, ads and to protect the app from abuse (Firebase App Check with Play Integrity)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To count steps and show goals, streaks, history and statistics
  • To run friend leagues, groups, group journeys, challenges, achievements and friend updates
  • To let you add friends by friend code, QR code, invite link or email
  • To show weather relevant to your location and start journeys near you
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure and prevent abuse
  • To show ads to users without Premium, and to provide Premium to subscribers
  • To understand which features help people keep walking, and to fix crashes

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We use these services to run the app:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore database, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config and App Check.
  • Google Sign-In: to let you sign in with your Google account.
  • Google Play Billing: to process subscriptions.
  • Health Connect (Google): if you turn it on, we read your step data to improve accuracy. We do not write to Health Connect.
  • Open-Meteo: weather forecasts for your approximate location. No account or API key is used.

Advertising. Users without Premium may see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, and uses them for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention under Google's policies. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Other users. Signed-in users can see your display name, level and step totals on leaderboards, leagues and groups, depending on your privacy settings. Your email address is used to let people who already know it add you as a friend.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Permissions We Request

  • Physical activity: to count steps with the device sensor
  • Approximate location (optional): for weather, rain reminders and journeys near you
  • Health Connect, read steps (optional): to import step data
  • Notifications (optional): for reminders and friend updates. Each type can be turned off in Settings
  • Internet and network: to sync data, load weather and show ads
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob and Firebase Analytics

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Choose who sees your activity and leaderboard presence in Settings → Privacy (everyone, friends only or nobody).
  • Use Stepory without an account; your steps then stay on your phone.
  • Turn off Health Connect, weather, and each notification type in Settings.
  • Export your data from your Profile at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account). See how to delete your account for what is deleted and kept.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or other regions with similar laws, you may have additional rights (access, correction, restriction, portability, objection). To exercise them, contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, step history, settings, friends, friend requests, friend updates, challenges and your steps in groups are deleted from our database straight away. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for up to 14 months and are not linked to your name or email. Copies in Google's backups are removed within their normal backup cycle.

7. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We use Firebase security rules, App Check and secure sign-in to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children

Stepory is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
